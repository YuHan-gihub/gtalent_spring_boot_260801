在aws上修改 (貼上公鑰在後面行)
nano authorized_keys


修改私鑰權限 用最高權限powershell
takeown /f "D:\aws\gtalent_actions_ed25519_26092201"
icacls "D:\aws\gtalent_actions_ed25519_26092201" /reset
icacls "D:\aws\gtalent_actions_ed25519_26092201" /grant:r "${env:USERNAME}:(R)"
icacls "D:\aws\gtalent_actions_ed25519_26092201" /inheritance:r
icacls "D:\aws\gtalent_actions_ed25519_26092201"
